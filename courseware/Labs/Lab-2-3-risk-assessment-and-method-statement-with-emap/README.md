# Lab 2.3 Risk assessment and method statement with EMAP

IES | 20 minutes | LO2, LO3

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. Read work-activity.txt. This is a desk exercise: no physical task may be performed. Identify missing chemical identity, SDS and isolation information.

2. In Word, build an E-MAP prompt: engineering context, measurable review criteria, explicit assumptions, and parameters or boundaries. Use the prompt below.

3. Generate a planning risk table with hazard, affected person, consequence, existing evidence, proposed controls, missing information and reviewer. Use qualitative risk discussion unless an approved rating matrix is supplied.

4. Generate a method-statement planning outline containing prerequisites, hold points, references to approved site procedures, emergency planning and handback. Reject invented operational isolation or PPE instructions.

5. Use hazard-checklist.csv to challenge the draft. Ask for missed hazards and contradictory assumptions. Save both draft versions and an unresolved-items list; mark release blocked pending competent review.

## Acceptance checks

- All seven supplied hazard categories are addressed.
- Missing SDS and isolation information block release.
- Risk controls are proposals and not approved instructions.
