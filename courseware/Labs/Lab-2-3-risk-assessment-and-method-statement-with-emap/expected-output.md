# Expected output example

Illustrative teaching example, not a recorded Copilot run or an approved engineering result.

Chemical exposure: substance unidentified; SDS required; chemical risk reviewer to confirm controls. Hold point: do not proceed to work planning approval until identity and compatibility evidence are available.

## Acceptance checks

- All seven supplied hazard categories are addressed.
- Missing SDS and isolation information block release.
- Risk controls are proposals and not approved instructions.

## Worked planning risk assessment excerpt

| Hazard | Possible consequence | Evidence gap | Proposed planning control | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electrical energy | Injury from unexpected energy | Approved isolation plan absent | Competent person to establish and verify the approved arrangement | Hold |
| Pressurised liquid | Uncontrolled release | Line inventory and pressure unknown | Obtain approved depressurisation and containment plan | Hold |
| Chemical exposure | Harm depends on substance | Identity and SDS missing | Identify chemical and review substance-specific controls | Hold |
| Unexpected start | Movement during planned work | Control-system state unknown | Review approved prevention and verification arrangements | Hold |
| Spill | Environmental impact | Drainage and response plan absent | Review containment and approved response route | Hold |
| Access and lifting | Handling or access injury | Survey and lifting assessment missing | Competent assessment before work planning approval | Hold |
| Restart | Unsafe handback | Test and witness plan absent | Define authorised test and handback evidence | Hold |

**Method-statement planning outline:** scope; competent roles; required references; evidence prerequisites; approval hold point; references to approved task procedures; emergency and spill arrangements; verification and handback. All physical work remains unauthorised. No likelihood or severity score is invented without an applicable risk matrix.
