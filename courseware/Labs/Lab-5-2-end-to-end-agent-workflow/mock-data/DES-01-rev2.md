## Clause 2 Duty
Nominal delivery is 50 m3/h at 30 m head.
## Clause 3 Classroom acceptance
The teaching acceptance band is plus or minus 5 percent of nominal delivery, hence 47.5 to 52.5 m3/h. Record three readings at agreed duty, instrument identity and witness. This is not a published engineering standard.
