# Multi-Persona Review Protocol

Fictional training reference for structured self-challenge.

## The three lenses

- **Reliability engineer** - failure mechanisms, evidence quality, what the data cannot rule out.
- **Operations lead** - practicality, production impact, what the change costs to run.
- **Data-quality reviewer** - sample size, measurement integrity, missing values, definitions.

## Procedure

1. Give each lens an initial position independently. Do not converge yet.
2. Run two rounds. In each, every lens raises one specific challenge and gives one evidence-backed response.
3. Cite supplied record IDs throughout. State unknowns as unknowns.
4. A moderator closes with supported conclusions, unresolved disagreements, evidence still needed, and a draft recommendation.

## Honesty rules

Do not invent facts to win a point. Do not force consensus; preserved disagreement is a valid outcome and often the useful one. State plainly that these are simulated perspectives generated by one model, not independent experts, and not an approval.
