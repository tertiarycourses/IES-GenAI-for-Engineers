# Lab 4.3 Minutes to action tracker to report

IES | 15 minutes | LO4

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. Open meeting-minutes.txt in Word or approved Teams meeting content. In Copilot, extract action IDs, descriptions, owners, due dates, status, evidence and blockers into a table.

2. Compare against the source: A03 appears twice and must remain one action. A04 lacks owner and date; retain TBD. Copy the reviewed table to Excel and save as a workbook.

3. Pass the reviewed tracker, with source version, to IES Report Writer. If Lab 4.2 is unavailable, use its instructions in Copilot Chat and label the run a manual fallback.

4. Check that the report preserves open actions and does not authorise procurement. Save the minutes, reviewed tracker and report with a handoff record.

5. Describe a recurring design: scheduled trigger, approved input location, extraction, human review, draft report and release approval. Record owner, duplicate detection by action ID, failure notification and retry rules. This lab runs the chain interactively; scheduling needs separately configured tenant tooling and permissions.

## Acceptance checks

- Four unique actions and A04 has TBD owner and date.
- The report retains the approval blocker.
- Interactive execution is distinguished from an unattended scheduled flow.

## If access is unavailable

Use approved paired access or the local source files to prepare a draft and configuration blueprint. Record live agent or permission tests as not executed. Do not use personal accounts to bypass organisational controls.
