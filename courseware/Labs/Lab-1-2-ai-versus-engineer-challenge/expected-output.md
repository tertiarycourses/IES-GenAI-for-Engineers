# Expected output example

Illustrative teaching example, not a recorded Copilot run or an approved engineering result.

Observation: 32 m3/h at 09:12, returning to 47 m3/h at 09:20. Hypothesis: measurement or process disturbance, not a confirmed defect. Request: suction pressure and calibration evidence. Decision: responsible engineer to review.

## Acceptance checks

- Facts match the notes and unknowns stay unknown.
- Timing includes human review and the brief has a decision owner.

## Worked technical brief

**Context:** PS-17 reported intermittent indicated delivery on 17 August 2026. The duty pump was running; standby availability is unconfirmed.

**Observations:** Indicated flow fell from 48 to 32 m3/h at 09:12 and returned to 47 m3/h at 09:20. The discharge gauge read 3.1 bar at 09:15. Vibration was reported without a measured value.

**Hypotheses:** A process disturbance or measurement issue could explain the indication. The available notes do not distinguish these possibilities.

**Missing evidence:** Suction pressure, calibration record, relevant work order, measured vibration and standby status.

**Decision request:** The responsible engineer should review those records before deciding the next approved investigation. Pump replacement is an unverified suggestion, not a released action.
