# Lab 3.1 FMEA document generation

IES | 30 minutes | LO1, LO2, LO3

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. Read the system description and classroom rating scale. Open fmea-template.csv in Excel and save a working copy as an Excel workbook in approved storage. Keep the source CSV unchanged.

2. Format the data as a table with headers. Use Copilot to review function, failure mode, effect, cause and current control. Keep current controls separate from proposed actions.

3. Inspect S, O and D ratings and add a justification for each. In cell J2 enter =G2*H2*I2 and fill down to J4, or use the equivalent structured-reference formula for the table.

4. Sort RPN largest to smallest while selecting the whole table. Verify F01=192, F02=189 and F03=175 manually. Also review severity 9 in F02 even though it is not the largest RPN.

5. Ask for action, owner, due date and evidence required for each row. Do not reduce residual ratings simply because an action is proposed.

6. In Word, draft a one-page FMEA review summary with scope, top findings, unresolved evidence, action ownership and a blank professional review decision. Save the workbook and summary.

## Acceptance checks

- RPNs are 192, 189 and 175; complete rows remain aligned after sorting.
- Ratings have stated justifications and high severity is reviewed.
- Summary remains a draft awaiting review.
