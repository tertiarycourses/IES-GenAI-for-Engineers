# Lab 2.2 Site notes to an engineering report

IES | 15 minutes | LO2, LO3

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. Read site-notes.txt and highlight timestamps, measurements, reported observations and missing evidence. Create a Word inspection report.

2. Paste or reference the notes in Copilot and use the prompt below. Request separate observations, findings, potential non-conformances, recommendations and an executive summary.

3. Check that a suspected seal leak is not presented as confirmed. A non-conformance needs a cited requirement; where one is missing, use potential issue requiring review.

4. Ask for a plain-language executive summary of no more than 100 words. Preserve the uncertainty and avoid an instruction to start maintenance.

5. Save the report, source reference and two corrections made during review. Exchange reports with a neighbour and trace one measurement back to its timestamp.

## Acceptance checks

- All four timestamps and measurements are preserved.
- The liquid identity and calibration status remain unresolved.

Use approved paired access or the local source files to prepare a draft and configuration blueprint. Record live agent or permission tests as not executed. Do not use personal accounts to bypass organisational controls.
