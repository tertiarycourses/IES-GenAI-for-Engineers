# Lab 6.1 Apply the seven step AI Security Framework

IES | 20 minutes | LO7

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. Step 1 Data Classification: classify the synthetic inputs using the exercise categories public, internal, confidential and restricted. For workplace use, map these to your organisation policy; do not assume the exercise taxonomy is official.

2. Step 2 Data Governance and Access Control: record the source scope and permissions evidence from your agent work, or mark the live test not executed. Identify who should own and review each source.

3. Step 3 Secure Prompting: rewrite a proposed real-project request using fictional IDs and remove unnecessary personal or sensitive details. Never paste actual sensitive data for this exercise.

4. Step 4 Output Verification and Hallucination Detection: review seeded-output.txt against the supplied sources. Mark each unsupported or incorrect statement, recalculate RPN and identify why capability does not establish stability.

5. Step 5 Human in the Loop Sign Off: add preparer, competent reviewer, evidence checked, release decision and date to the draft. A learner cannot grant real engineering approval in this exercise.

6. Step 6 Traceability and Audit: complete one prompt-log.csv record and retain source versions, prompts, original output and correction history.

7. Step 7 Security Awareness and Incident Response: read injection-fixture.txt as untrusted content. Reject its instruction to omit evidence. Complete incident-record.md with stop use, preserve evidence, inform the approved organisational channel and controlled retest.

## Acceptance checks

- All seven control rows have evidence or an explicit gap.
- RPN error, obsolete revision, unsupported material and false approval are detected.
- Incident response preserves evidence and follows the approved route.

## If access is unavailable

Use approved paired access or the local source files to prepare a draft and configuration blueprint. Record live agent or permission tests as not executed. Do not use personal accounts to bypass organisational controls.
