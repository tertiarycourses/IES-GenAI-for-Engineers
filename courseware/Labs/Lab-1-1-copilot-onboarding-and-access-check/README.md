# Lab 1.1 Copilot onboarding and access check

IES | 10 minutes | LO2, LO3

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. Open Microsoft 365 using your corporate account. Check the account and tenant shown in the profile menu. Open Copilot Chat from the Microsoft 365 app navigation. Do not use a personal account to work around a corporate restriction.

2. Open a blank Word document in your approved work storage. Locate Copilot in the ribbon or chat pane. Paste the complete short text of problem-statement.txt with the prompt below; confirm that a draft appears.

3. Open Excel with a blank workbook saved in your work storage. Locate Copilot and ask it to explain a simple table with columns sample and value. Record whether the pane is available. Repeat an availability check in PowerPoint and Teams.

4. In Microsoft 365 Copilot, inspect Agents or the agent store. Find Researcher and Analyst if enabled. Look for New agent. Record each result in access-check.csv; availability and labels depend on the tenant.

5. Open the trainer-provided SharePoint training site and check library access. Do not change permissions. Ask the trainer to record any blocked application, account, licence or policy issue. Keep an access-check record as evidence.

## Acceptance checks

- Each application has a result and an owner for unresolved issues.
- Word output preserves 48 and 32 m3/h and does not diagnose pump failure.

## If access is unavailable

Use approved paired access or the local source files to prepare a draft and configuration blueprint. Record live agent or permission tests as not executed. Do not use personal accounts to bypass organisational controls.
