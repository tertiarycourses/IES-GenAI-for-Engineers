# Lab 2.1 Technical specification with RISE

IES | 20 minutes | LO2, LO3

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. Open design-requirements.csv and DES-01-rev2.md. Review confirmed requirements versus gaps. Create a Word document in approved storage named Lab-2.1-Specification.

2. In the Copilot pane attach or reference the approved source files if available; otherwise paste their short text. State the source names explicitly. Use the R.I.S.E. prompt below.

3. Review the draft section by section. Check nominal flow 50 m3/h, head 30 m and the teaching tolerance. Keep wetted material, supply voltage and ambient conditions as TBD.

4. Ask Copilot to produce a requirement-to-test checklist with IDs, values, units, source clause, evidence and status. Independently compute 50 x 0.95 and 50 x 1.05; compare with the acceptance band.

5. Run the verification prompt in prompts.md. Correct any invented material or standard. Save the specification and checklist with a draft status and reviewer field.

## Acceptance checks

- Teaching band is 47.5-52.5 m3/h.
- Every requirement cites its source or is marked missing.
- No invented material, voltage or approval.
