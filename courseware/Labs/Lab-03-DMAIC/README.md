# Copilot in Excel: DMAIC and improvement reporting

90 minutes | IES | Original synthetic CW-401 engineering case. Values and limits are fictional teaching assumptions, not plant operating instructions. Generated references are independently calculated examples, not recorded Copilot executions.

Open Starter.xlsx. Lab-Handout.pdf contains the full procedure; prompts.md and the workbook Prompts sheet contain the prompts. Attempt the lab before opening Worked-Reference.xlsx. All source files are local in mock-data.

## 1 Define the problem

Open WeeklyEvents and DefectCounts. These are an eight-week event register, separate from the120-test engineering investigation sample. Define the CTQ as acceptance deviations per logged test opportunity. It is not treatment efficacy or regulatory compliance.

## 2 Calculate exposure-aware rates

Submit DMAIC1. Sum baseline weeks1–6:93 deviations/1008 opportunities=9.22619%. Pilot weeks7–8:11/336=3.27381%. Label phase and denominator. A comparison with unequal observation periods should use rates, not raw count reduction.

## 3 Create the Pareto

Submit DMAIC2. Counts55,16,12,10 sum to93. Cumulative shares59.14%,76.34%,89.25%,100%. Use descending columns with a cumulative-percent line on a secondary axis. Categories prioritise investigation and do not establish root causes.

## 4 Interpret change honestly

Absolute reduction=5.95238 percentage points; relative reduction=64.51613%. The pilot has only two weeks, and strainer replacement plus verification occurred together. Do not call the result proof of one cause, permanent improvement or quantified financial savings.

## 5 Link Analyse to Improve

Use the SPC evidence and FMEA action list to propose a follow-up study: agreed CTQ, fixed test conditions, documented instrument check, comparable baseline/pilot scope and independent engineering review. Mark proposal status; do not prescribe hazardous operations.

## 6 Write the Control plan

Submit DMAIC3. Specify the metric, source, review frequency, responsible role, signal rule and escalation route under approved site procedures. Keep baseline limits fixed until a justified re-baseline is reviewed. Retain ACT-401-04 as open pending evidence.

## 7 Present one workbook summary

Prepare six cells: problem, baseline, pilot, evidence, limitations and proposed next decision. Save the rates, Pareto, DMAIC and ControlPlan sheets. Verify every management claim back to a source row and give a two-minute review to a partner.
