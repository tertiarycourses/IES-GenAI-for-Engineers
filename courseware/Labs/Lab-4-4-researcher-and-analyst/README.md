# Lab 4.4 Researcher and Analyst

IES | 15 minutes | LO5

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. In Microsoft 365 Copilot, select Researcher from Agents if available. Paste research-question.txt. Require sources supporting each comparison claim and record scope limitations.

2. Open two cited source links. Check publisher, title, date or revision and whether the source actually supports the associated statement. Catalogue pages do not provide full standard clauses.

3. Select Analyst and attach process-data.csv and defects.csv. Use the prompt below. Require baseline and monitoring separation and visible calculations.

4. Compare Analyst results with the formula method in this folder expected-output.md. Challenge any claim that a signal proves a particular root cause.

5. Save a research comparison, two verified citations and the analysis with a correction log. If either agent is unavailable, run a paired demonstration or manual source analysis and record the missing live capability.

## Acceptance checks

- At least two citations are opened and checked.
- Sample 33 is detected and baseline statistics match the reference.
