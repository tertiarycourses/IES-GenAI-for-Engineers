# GenAI for Engineers - three guided Copilot in Excel labs

IES | One-day course | 65 minutes total

Follow the engineering methods in the same order in every course artifact:

1. **Lab 1 - FMEA** - 4 failure modes - 20 minutes
2. **Lab 2 - DMAIC** - 6 weekly records - 20 minutes
3. **Lab 3 - SPC** - 12 pump readings - 25 minutes

Each lab has one small source table, one RISE prompt, one independent check, and one worked reference. Use `Starter.xlsx` first. Open `Worked-Reference.xlsx` only after checking your Copilot draft.
