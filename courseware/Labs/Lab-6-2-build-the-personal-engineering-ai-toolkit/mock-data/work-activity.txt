RAMS-01 revision 1 | classroom desk exercise only
Prepare a planning-stage risk assessment for replacement of a fictional dosing-pump assembly. Possible energy sources: electrical, pressurised liquid and stored mechanical energy. Chemical identity, SDS, isolation points and line inventory are deliberately missing. No physical work is authorised. Work location is not a confined space in this case, but site classification is unverified. Draft must identify competent-person review, isolation verification, chemical compatibility, spill response, access and emergency planning as prerequisites. Do not invent PPE, exposure limits, live-work steps or isolation arrangements.
