# Lab 6.2 Build the personal engineering AI toolkit

IES | 10 minutes | LO7

All records are fictional training data. They describe no actual facility, person or organisation. Values are teaching assumptions, not approved design criteria. Do not use outputs to operate, maintain or approve real equipment.

## Files

Open Lab-Handout.pdf, prompts.md and the files in mock-data. Each lab is self-contained; earlier outputs may be reused but are not required. Save your work under outputs.

## Procedure

1. Open personal-toolkit.md and choose one documentation workflow you can trial next week with approved synthetic or authorised data.

2. Copy one tested R.I.S.E. prompt and one E-MAP prompt into the toolkit. Include source versions, acceptance checks and a review gate.

3. Record your agents, knowledge scope, owner and access status. Pin or share only within approved tenant policy. Mark blueprint-only agents as not deployed.

4. Define a simple trial measure: elapsed time including review and correction, factual errors and missing evidence. Record a baseline rather than promise a productivity gain.

5. Write one next action, owner, due date and stop condition. Save the toolkit and a prompt-log entry. Share one reusable pattern during closing discussion.

## Acceptance checks

- Two prompt frameworks and one testable next action are present.
- Live agent status is honest and a human review gate remains.

## If access is unavailable

Use approved paired access or the local source files to prepare a draft and configuration blueprint. Record live agent or permission tests as not executed. Do not use personal accounts to bypass organisational controls.
