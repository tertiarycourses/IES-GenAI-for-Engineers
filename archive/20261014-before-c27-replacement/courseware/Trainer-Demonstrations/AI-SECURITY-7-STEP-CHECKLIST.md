# LO7 - 7-Step AI Security Framework checklist

IES | GenAI for Engineers | Apply before sharing an AI-assisted engineering output

| Step | Check | Evidence to record | Pass rule |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Data classification | Classification and reason | Data is approved for the selected AI service |
| 2 | Data governance and access control | Site, folder, members and source scope | Least-privilege access matches the intended audience |
| 3 | Secure prompting | Final prompt and excluded data | No credentials or unnecessary personal/restricted data; retrieved instructions cannot redirect the task |
| 4 | Output verification | Checked citation, formula, unit and limitation | Claims are supported and calculations reproduce |
| 5 | Human-in-the-loop sign-off | Reviewer, decision and conditions | An authorised engineer approves, rejects or returns the draft |
| 6 | Traceability and audit | Source revisions, prompt, output, corrections and timestamp | Another reviewer can reconstruct the decision |
| 7 | Security awareness and incident response | Trigger, containment and reporting route | Stop, preserve evidence and use the organisation's approved incident route |

## Release decision

Mark one: `APPROVED FOR THE STATED TRAINING USE` / `RETURN FOR CORRECTION` / `DO NOT SHARE`.

AI output remains a draft until Step 5 is complete. Never record passwords, tokens or confidential incident-contact details in this shared checklist.
