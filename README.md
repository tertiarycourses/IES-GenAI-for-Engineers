# IES GenAI for Engineers

## One-day Copilot in Excel workshop — v2.0

**Three connected labs, one realistic synthetic engineering case.**

![IES workshop preview](screenshot.png)

| Lab | Focus | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| [01 SPC](courseware/Labs/Lab-01-SPC/README.md) | Data audit, formulas, control chart and interpretation |140 min|
| [02 FMEA](courseware/Labs/Lab-02-FMEA/README.md) | Failure analysis, RPN, severity review and actions |100 min|
| [03 DMAIC](courseware/Labs/Lab-03-DMAIC/README.md) | Rates, Pareto, improvement proposal and control plan |90 min|

The day runs 09:00–17:00: 330 lab minutes, 30 setup and 30 final review; 60-minute lunch and two 15-minute tea breaks. This user-directed focus replaces the broader original 16-lab plan. Earlier materials are retained locally outside the current learner package.

Each lab has **Starter.xlsx, Worked-Reference.xlsx, a PDF handout, CSV source data and prompts**. Prompts are in the workbook's Prompts sheet and in prompts.md. Attempt the task before opening the reference. All three folders are self-contained.

CW-401 includes 120 unique delivery tests, three seeded quality issues, linked maintenance evidence, six provisional FMEA rows and an eight-week deviation log. Specifications and ratings are fictional teaching assumptions. Worked files contain recalculated formulas and charts; they are not recorded Copilot executions.

Use an approved work/school account and training storage with Copilot in Excel. Actual access depends on your tenant. Use the Excel Copilot pane to analyse tables, propose formulas and create charts; independently verify results. Label manual fallback work honestly.

IES is the training provider. The facilitator copy is supplied separately and excluded from this learner repository. The reference ebook is not redistributed. [Sources and prompt provenance](courseware/REFERENCES.md).
